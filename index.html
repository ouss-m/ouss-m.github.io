01 / THE RESEARCHER

Web Application Security Researcher

Bug bounty research since 2024 · Responsible disclosures across global platforms

I'm a Computer Science graduate from Beirut Arab University focused on web application security, penetration testing, and bug bounty research. Through HackerOne, I've responsibly disclosed vulnerabilities to global organizations including Porsche, DoorDash, Deriv, and Krisp. My work covers the OWASP Top 10 and beyond: broken access control, XSS, server-side template injection, SSRF, security misconfigurations, authentication and session issues, CSRF, caching problems, and business logic flaws. I keep my knowledge of these categories current as new techniques and classes emerge.

I care about finding the flaws that matter and explaining them clearly, so engineering teams can fix them quickly. Alongside hands-on testing, I research AI-assisted security workflows for scoped, privacy-aware testing.
